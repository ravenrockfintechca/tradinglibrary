## Research candidates

### QSI-101 | Net Operating Assets Anomaly
- **Alpha:** Cumulative difference between operating accounting profit and cash-generation can cause overvaluation when investors focus on reported earnings. `NOA=(operating assets-operating liabilities)/lagged assets` is a *simplified research primitive*, not the full accounting mapping.
- **Signal/entry/exit:** At least 4 months after fiscal-year end or actual filing date (later of the two), rank NOA within industries; long low-NOA quintile, investigate short high-NOA; exit at next monthly rebalance, delisting or 2% single-name limit. **Market/frequency:** US stocks, annual statements/monthly portfolios. **Parameters/risk:** 20/80 quantiles, sector/size neutral, minimum $5m ADV, max 1% ADV participation; accounting restatements, financial-firm classifications, value exposure.
- **Paper:** Hirshleifer, Hou, Teoh, Zhang (2004), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacceco.2004.10.002; authors' page https://sites.uci.edu/dhirshle/abstracts/do-investors-overvalue-firms-with-bloated-balance-sheets/. 1964–2002 sample: NOA negatively predicts long-run returns; net Sharpe/DD unverified.
- **Data/stack:** SEC XBRL PIT balance sheet, corporate actions, CRSP-equivalent delisting data; Python/pandas, PostgreSQL, Z-QCC deterministic ranking. **GitHub:** https://github.com/OpenSourceAP/CrossSection (general factor library, not verified exact reproduction; GPL-2.0, not archived, pushed 2026-10-27).
- **Institution / exact strategy AUM:** Adoption of this exact rule not verified / UNKNOWN. **Costs/capacity/bias:** spread, borrow, accounting lag, survivorship, restatements, small-cap impact; exclude banks unless accounting mapping is tailored.
- **Deployment:** PIT filings -> NOA mapping audit -> sector ranks -> cost/impact model -> walk-forward -> DRAFT_NO_SALE. **Priority:** P1; strong small-capital *screening* use. **Mechanism overlap:** QSI-080 Accruals: related accounting persistence, but cumulative NOA stock rather than single-period accrual flow.

### QSI-102 | Organization Capital Risk Premium
- **Alpha:** Firm-specific know-how embodied in labor makes cash-flow claims shared with key talent; higher organizational capital may carry a distinct risk premium. `OrgCap_t=(1-delta)OrgCap_(t-1)+SGA_t`, rank `OrgCap/assets` within industry. Capitalization needs initial-stock assumptions, inflation adjustment and SG&A classification.
- **Signal/entry/exit:** Long top 20% of industry-relative OrgCap/assets, low bottom 20% research short; annual filing-lagged signal, monthly rebalance. **Markets:** US common stocks. **Risk/parameters:** illustrative 15% depreciation, multi-year SG&A initialization, sector/size neutral, 2% name cap, 1% ADV participation; labor mobility and intangible-asset measurement risk.
- **Paper:** Eisfeldt & Papanikolaou (2013), DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12034. Publisher reports ~4.6% average-return spread; Northwestern summary ~4.7% annually; RePEc working-paper abstract reports long-short Sharpe 0.57. These are historical study metrics, NOT current costed results. Sources https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jofi.12034 and https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v68y2013i4p1365-1406.html.
- **Data/stack:** SEC XBRL PIT SG&A, total assets, sector classifications, deflators, corporate actions; Python/pandas, PostgreSQL, Z-QCC factor engine. **GitHub:** https://github.com/OpenSourceAP/CrossSection (general factor library; exact replication not checked; GPL-2.0, not archived, pushed 2026-10-27).
- **Institution / exact strategy AUM:** Not verified / UNKNOWN. **Costs/capacity/bias:** turnover, sector concentration, intangible measurement, retrospective SG&A, point-in-time filings, impact.
- **Deployment:** SG&A PIT -> perpetual inventory -> industry ranks -> risk neutralization -> costed walk-forward. **Priority:** P0 research engine; high for small-capital long-only screen. **Mechanism overlap:** QSI-071 Quality and QSI-062 Profitability are different from organizational-capital embodied labor rents.

### QSI-103 | Convertible Bond Delta-Hedged Arbitrage
- **Alpha:** Convertible value combines credit bond and equity conversion option; investigate theoretical mispricing while delta-hedging equity. `short_shares=CB_units*conversion_ratio*equity_delta`. This is a hedge-size primitive, NOT an arbitrage valuation model.
- **Signal/entry/exit:** Long convertible when model fair value minus executable offer exceeds funding, short-stock borrow, hedging, credit and liquidity buffers; short shares to hedge delta; exit on spread convergence, credit trigger, recall or drawdown. **Markets/frequency:** US/JP convertibles and equities; bond quotes daily/intraday, credit and option surfaces daily. **Risk:** issuer default, equity gap, gamma, hard-to-borrow, corporate actions, forced liquidation, call/put clauses.
- **Paper:** Hutchinson & Gallagher (2010), DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-5957.2009.02178.x. Simulated portfolio has default/term-structure factor exposures; negative abnormal returns in 2007–08 crisis hedge fund samples. Supporting https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/48692/ (2009 convertible arbitrage issuance underpricing) and https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/393868/ (2024 liquidation risk).
- **Data/stack:** bond indentures, dealer quotes, yield curves, credit spreads, options, borrow/stock PIT; Python + QuantLib + PostgreSQL + Z-QCC Greeks. **GitHub:** https://github.com/lballabio/QuantLib (pricing toolkit, not complete paper reproduction; not archived, pushed 2026-11-13; API license NOASSERTION, review LICENSE file).
- **Institution / exact strategy AUM:** Academic hedge-fund samples, no independently identified current exact-rule manager / UNKNOWN. **Cost/capacity:** CB dealer spreads, low depth, repo, equity short and recall; historical bond quote bias, stale pricing.
- **Deployment:** terms parser -> credit/equity valuation -> delta/credit stress -> execution-cost simulation -> research only. **Priority:** P2; low small-capital feasibility. **Mechanism overlap:** QSI-095 Embedded Leverage prices leverage demand, not convertible credit-option replication.

### QSI-104 | FX 25-Delta Risk-Reversal Forecast
- **Alpha:** FX OTM call-vs-put IV asymmetry encodes relative tail-demand and risk-neutral skew; test predictive value for subsequent spot returns. `RR25=IV(25delta call)-IV(25delta put)`. Quote sign depends on currency pair orientation and market convention.
- **Signal/entry/exit:** Use lagged 1m 25-delta RR, spot momentum and rate differential as inputs to rolling expanding-window predictive model; trade only when OOS predicted excess return exceeds spread, financing and model uncertainty; exit after one day or risk stop. **Markets/frequency:** EURUSD, USDJPY, G10 FX; daily options quotes and spot, daily model forecast. **Parameters/risk:** 1m tenor, 25 delta, 2y training minimum, vol-target, no leverage initially, event-risk limits; avoid same-day post-close quote leakage.
- **Paper:** Ratcliff (2009), DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/ijfe.393; sample Jan 2002–Jun 2004, options skew improves out-of-sample next-day EUR/USD forecasts vs random walk and interest parity; NOT a verified trading Sharpe. Related Carr & Wu (2007) DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2006.03.010.
- **Data/stack:** licensed FX 25-delta vol surface, spot, forwards, rates, bid/ask; Python/sklearn, PostgreSQL, Z-QCC FX cost engine. **GitHub:** https://github.com/lballabio/QuantLib (FX option pricing support, not paper reproduction; not archived, pushed 2026-11-13).
- **Institution / exact strategy AUM:** Not verified / UNKNOWN. **Costs/capacity/bias:** expensive historical vol surface, stale/misaligned fixing, spread, spot financing, convention mismatches, model selection bias.
- **Deployment:** FX vol quote convention -> RR25 PIT -> rolling forecasts -> OOS direction/cost test -> research signal. **Priority:** P1; medium small-capital as signal. **Mechanism overlap:** QSI-089 equity call-put IV signal targets stocks, QSI-104 uses FX options to forecast spot currency.

### QSI-105 | PV01-Neutral Yield-Curve Butterfly
- **Alpha:** Compare belly yield with PV01-adjusted wings to research curve curvature and carry/convexity; `curvature=y_mid-w_short*y_short-w_long*y_long` is a geometric signal ONLY. Real trade weights must neutralize PV01 and address annuity coordinates, funding and roll-down.
- **Signal/entry/exit:** Fit PIT curve and z-score residual curvature by tenor (e.g. 2y/5y/10y); only investigate mean reversion after historical cost-adjusted validation; exit on curvature normalization or risk limits. **Markets/frequency:** USD Treasury or swaps, daily; 1y rolling estimation, weekly rebalance. **Risk:** steepening/flattening, basis, convexity, margin, dealer spreads, PV01 drift.
- **Paper:** Sun (2026), first published 2026-08-09, DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/fut.70136. Paper explicitly cautions instrument-parallel-shift convexity is not a trading law: 0.25bp full bid-ask benchmark makes mean and median round-trip results negative. Source https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/fut.70136.
- **Data/stack:** Treasury yields, instrument prices, swap discount curves, PV01, dealer bid/ask; Python/QuantLib, PostgreSQL, Z-QCC curve math. **GitHub:** https://github.com/lballabio/QuantLib (general fixed-income toolkit; no exact study replication verified, not archived, pushed 2026-11-13).
- **Institution / exact strategy AUM:** Not verified / UNKNOWN. **Costs/capacity/bias:** dealer bid-ask, hedging, margin, interpolation, nonparallel shocks; quote mid is not executable.
- **Deployment:** PIT curve -> bootstrap -> PV01-neutral weights -> scenario risk -> realistic costed OOS test. **Priority:** P2; unsuitable small-capital trading, useful research. **Mechanism overlap:** QSI-083 cash-futures basis is different from swap/Treasury curve curvature.
