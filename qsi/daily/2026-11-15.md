### QSI-106 | Cross-Sectional Crash Tail-Risk Beta
- **Category / mechanism:** tail_risk / cross_sectional_extreme_negative_return_common_tail_factor_exposure
- **Formula:** `beta_tail=Cov(stock_return,lagged_common_tail_factor)/Var(lagged_common_tail_factor)`
- **Signal:** Estimate downside-tail factor from cross-sectional crashes, lagged tail beta; rank exposures monthly; first use risk filter
- **Paper DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhu039
- **Empirical:** Original paper: high minus low tail-risk loading portfolios differ by 5.4% annual three-factor alpha, historical, not costed strategy
- **Rules, data, risk, deployment:** Kelly & Jiang (2014), common crash-tail risk factor estimated from extreme negative stock returns. **Not** a simple downside standard deviation. Research-only proxy: aggregate cross-sectional negative-tail quantile each date, estimate rolling stock beta to that lagged factor. **Entry:** use lowest tail-exposure names as initial risk filter; research high-minus-low exposure as priced-risk premium, not assume safe direction. **Exit:** monthly refresh, volatility or liquidity guard. **Data:** CRSP-equivalent PIT stock returns/delistings, daily; factor monthly; 36-60m beta window. **Risks:** tail beta unstable in quiet regimes, crashes, stale constituents, nonlinear losses. **Evidence:** historical high minus low loading 5.4% annual three-factor alpha, not implementable net return. **Deployment:** PIT daily returns -> extreme-loss cross-section -> factor beta -> stress and costed OOS.

- **Formula:** `supplier_signal=sum(customer_sales_weight*lagged_customer_return)`
- **Signal:** Rank suppliers by major customers' lagged monthly returns, rebalance monthly, sector neutral
- **Paper DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01379.x
- **Empirical:** Original research long-short monthly alpha over 150 bps in historical sample; no verified post-cost Sharpe or max DD

Directed major-customer revenue relationships carry public customer information into supplier returns with lag. **Entry:** after disclosure is public and customer return month completed, rank supplier weighted lagged customer returns; long top quintile, short bottom only as research. **Exit:** monthly. **Data:** SEC 10-K major customer text, historical entity mapping, sales concentration, daily prices; monthly ranks. **Parameters:** customer revenue weights normalized, 12m disclosure expiry, min $5m ADV, <=1% ADV, <=2% issuer. **Risks:** unnamed customers, outdated links, supplier liquidity, large gap after news. **Evidence:** original long-short alpha >150bp/month historically, before current executable costs. **Deployment:** SEC relationship graph -> PIT edges -> customer returns -> supplier score -> Z-QCC walk-forward.

- **Formula:** `asset_growth=(total_assets_t-total_assets_t_minus_1)/total_assets_t_minus_1`
- **Signal:** Long lowest annual total asset growth quintile; high growth as short research or exclusion, rebalance monthly
- **Paper DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01370.x
- **Empirical:** Original study: asset growth predicts future abnormal returns including large caps; numeric costed Sharpe/DD not verified
- **Rules, data, risk, deployment:** Cooper, Gulen & Schill (2008). Corporate total-asset expansion predicts lower subsequent equity returns, distinct from accumulated NOA. **Entry:** rank annual asset growth within industries after actual filing release; low growth long and high growth research short. **Exit:** monthly refresh, financial restatement/merger exceptions. **Data:** SEC XBRL assets and fiscal dates, CRSP-equivalent delistings, monthly portfolios. **Parameters:** lagged fiscal-year total assets >0, 20/80 quintiles, 2% issuer, 1% ADV, industry neutral. **Risks:** M&A distortions, financials comparability, restatements, value/quality confounding. **Evidence:** original paper documents abnormal return prediction even among large caps, no verified numeric net Sharpe/DD. **Deployment:** PIT assets -> annual growth -> corporate-action adjustment -> ranks -> costed OOS.

### QSI-110 | Intangible Return Reversal
- **Category / mechanism:** behavioral_value / past_stock_return_orthogonal_to_accounting_fundamental_news_negative_predictor
- **Formula:** `intangible_return=historical_stock_return-model_predicted_return_from_past_accounting_performance`
- **Signal:** Train PIT fundamental news model on past data; monthly rank residual intangible return; low residual long research
- **Paper DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2006.00884.x
- **Empirical:** Original paper: future return negatively related to intangible past return; no verified numeric net Sharpe/DD
- **Rules, data, risk, deployment:** Daniel & Titman (2006). Historical price appreciation not explained by observable past fundamental accounting performance is an 'intangible' return component; high intangible historical return predicts lower future return. **Entry:** rolling historical model of past price returns using only then-public accounting news; long low residual and investigate high residual short. **Exit:** monthly. **Data:** PIT multi-year returns and fundamental changes, annual filings, delistings. **Parameters:** 3y trailing formation, monthly ranks, sector/size/valuation neutral; strict nested model training. **Risks:** double-dipping, model specification, momentum confounding, revisions. **Evidence:** original paper documents negative forecast relation; net Sharpe/DD unverified. **Deployment:** accounting-news PIT -> historical tangible model -> intangible residual -> walk-forward.
