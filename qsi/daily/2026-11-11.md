### QSI-086 | Open-market Share Repurchase Announcement Drift
**Category/alpha:** Equity event; delayed valuation adjustment after announced buyback authorization, particularly among value firms. **Research score:** `repurchase_fraction_of_shares * book_to_market`, a proxy rather than a calibrated return forecast.
**Entry/exit:** after publicly timestamped issuer/SEC announcement and next executable session; rank announced repurchase intensity conditional on valuation; investigate 6/12/24-month holding windows; exit on cancellation, risk or liquidity breach. Event signal, daily prices, monthly portfolio review.
**Parameters/risk:** ADV floor, issuer ≤3%, industry caps, verify authorization vs executed buyback, PIT financial statements, delisting returns, matching controls. **Data:** SEC 8-K, 10-Q, corporate actions, survivorship-free OHLCV. **Stack:** EDGAR → PIT events → primitive → Z-QCC cost/risk → walk-forward.
**Paper:** Ikenberry/Lakonishok/Vermaelen, *Market Underreaction to Open Market Share Repurchases*, NBER working paper DOI **10.3386/w4965**, https://www.nber.org/papers/w4965. Original 1980–1990 sample: **12.1% abnormal four-year buy-and-hold return**, **45.3%** among value stocks; neither annualized nor costed or replicated here.
**GitHub:** https://github.com/polakowo/vectorbt (generic framework, NOT paper replication; unarchived; last push observed 2026-11-08). Specific adopters/AUM unknown. Costs: event spreads, slippage, delayed filing, crowding; capacity conditional on issuer liquidity. **P0**, implement research-only SEC event monitor.

### QSI-087 | Commercial Hedger Pressure Futures Premium
**Category/alpha:** Futures positioning risk premium from commercial inventory hedging; separate from term-structure and momentum. **Signal:** `net_commercial_short/open_interest`; trade direction must be estimated per contract, not assumed universal.
**Entry/exit:** weekly CFTC COT release timestamp → expanding 156-week within-contract z-score → next tradable session, hold 4 weeks or pressure reversal; daily contract prices. **Risk:** roll/expiry, leverage, collateral, sector caps, 1% risk budget per contract; avoid look-ahead from report Tuesday as publication Friday.
**Data/stack:** CFTC COT PIT → futures prices/rolls and collateral → cross-sectional pressure → Z-QCC risk → costed backtest.
**Paper:** Bessembinder, *Systematic Risk, Hedging Pressure, and Risk Premiums in Futures Markets*, RFS 1992 DOI **10.1093/rfs/5.4.637**, https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/5.4.637. Evidence of hedger-position dependence in FX/agricultural futures; no verified strategy Sharpe/DD.
**GitHub:** https://github.com/pmorissette/bt (generic backtest framework, MIT, unarchived, last push 2026-11-08; not original-paper reproduction). Specific AUM/adopter unknown. Costs: spreads, roll, margin, liquidity. **P1**, micro-futures after contract-specific test.

### QSI-088 | Index Variance Risk Premium
**Category/alpha:** Volatility insurance premium; `IVAR - forecast_realized_variance` with matching units and tenor. Distinct from QSI-065 single-name cross-sectional option RV–IV strategy.
**Entry/exit:** evaluate 30-day index options strip and sell *bounded-risk* exposure only after estimated premium exceeds transaction/tail capital charge; exit expiry or risk stop. Daily signals, intraday fills. **Risk:** convex crash losses, vega, skew, margin, gap and settlement; avoid naked short variance for small capital.
**Data/stack:** SPX full OTM option chains, rates, dividends/forwards, underlying prices → no-arbitrage filters → model-free variance → forecast RV → scenario stress → Z-QCC.
**Paper:** Carr/Wu, *Variance Risk Premiums*, RFS 2009 DOI **10.1093/rfs/hhn038**, https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhn038; synthetic variance swap rates for five indexes and 35 stocks; no verified costed Sharpe or DD for this rule.
**GitHub:** https://github.com/lballabio/QuantLib (pricing infrastructure, not paper replication; unarchived, last push 2026-11-10). Specific AUM/adopter unknown. Costs: multi-leg spread, hedges, margin, jump loss; capacity limited by option chain and hedge depth. **P1** research, low small-capital suitability.

### QSI-089 | Put–Call Parity Implied Stock Return Signal
**Category/alpha:** Relative call-versus-put implied volatility may proxy informed directional option order flow, not mechanical arbitrage. **Signal:** `IV_call-IV_put` at matched strike/expiry, after dividends and quote checks.
**Entry/exit:** weekly long top quintile, avoid/short bottom quintile, next executable stock session; one-week holding. Daily option chain and stock prices. **Risk:** matched 30–60 DTE near-ATM, NBBO age, OI, spread, sector/beta neutrality, borrowing and stock liquidity.
**Data/stack:** PIT option NBBO → contract matching → IV inversion → ranks → stock execution cost → Z-QCC.
**Paper:** Cremers/Weinbaum, *Deviations from Put-Call Parity and Stock Return Predictability*, JFQA 2010 DOI **10.1017/S002210901000013X**, https://doi.org/10.1017/S002210901000013X. Original historical study reports **50bp/week** high-vs-low relative option expensiveness; NOT net executable return, and predictability decreased over sample.
**GitHub:** https://github.com/polakowo/vectorbt (generic framework, not paper replication; last push 2026-11-08). AUM/adopter unknown. Costs: option data, equity spread, short borrow; capacity limited by option liquidity. **P1** as signal filter.

### QSI-090 | Seasoned Equity Offering Long-Run Underperformance
**Category/alpha:** Primary seasoned equity issuance can reflect management timing of elevated valuations, predicting weak subsequent returns. Distinct from QSI-068 existing IPO share unlock and QSI-086 repurchases. **Signal:** `new_primary_shares/pre_issue_shares` plus issuance valuation and prior price run-up; a proxy, not estimated return.
**Entry/exit:** after verified SEO prospectus/closing timestamp, underweight/avoid high-dilution issuers; evaluate 6/12/24-month cohorts, quarterly rebalance; do not assume immediate post-offering drift. **Risk:** distinguish primary vs secondary sales and IPO, ATM issuance, industry/value/size matching, borrow, delistings, risk-based alternative explanations.
**Data/stack:** SEC S-3/424B/8-K → issuance event table → share-count dilution → matched nonissuer control → Z-QCC backtest.
**Paper:** Loughran/Ritter, *The New Issues Puzzle*, Journal of Finance 1995 DOI **10.1111/j.1540-6261.1995.tb05166.x**, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1995.tb05166.x. Original 1970–1990 sample: SEO firms average **7% annual return** in following five years vs **15%** matched nonissuers. Not a costed short-strategy result; competing risk-based explanations exist.
**GitHub:** https://github.com/OpenSourceAP/CrossSection (broad factor library, GPL-2.0, last push 2026-10-27; not verified as direct paper reproduction). Strategy AUM/adopter/Sharpe/DD unknown. Costs: spreads, borrow, multi-year capital, survivorship bias. **P1** for exclusion/risk monitor.
